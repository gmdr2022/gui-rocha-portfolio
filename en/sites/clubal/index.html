SITE 02 · ClubAL commercial website case

Live · three languages

ClubAL website

Product complexity presented through a direct journey with honest privacy and status.

This case study covers the website as a communication delivery. The ClubAL product page remains dedicated to the Windows software, its modules and deployment.

  • PT · EN · ES
  • Light + dark themes
  • No analytics

Explain a three-module product without overwhelming the first visit.

The content needed to present value, workflow and limits before technical detail, keeping the product understandable for visitors who do not know its architecture.

  • Problem before technology
  • Current status visible
  • Product and website kept distinct

The narrative follows the visitor’s decision.

The page starts with the operational outcome, shows how the three modules connect and leads into deployment, frequently asked questions and direct contact.

  • Information hierarchy
  • Anchor navigation
  • Context-specific calls to action

A published, localized experience with no tracking.

The website supports Portuguese, English and Spanish, light and dark themes, responsive layout, local assets and static publishing on Cloudflare Pages.

  • Three languages
  • Persistent themes
  • Explicit privacy
