Everyday use

No activation command. Open the project and work as usual.

C7ES operates as a Codex layer: it identifies context, applies proportional rigor and involves the responsible person only when a decision truly requires human authority.

Do I need to run a program or command to start C7?

No. Once installed, C7ES is loaded by Codex. Open a task in the correct project and describe the desired outcome; there is no launcher, executable or everyday ritual.

Do I need to call the Council of Seven?

No. The Council represents seven coordinated functional lenses, not seven bots or seven simultaneous answers. Quorum is automatic; “Full Council” only makes the perspectives visible when useful.

Does it work in ClubAL and other projects?

Yes. C7ES works across distinct projects without assuming a product. Local instructions and the repository’s real behavior continue to prevail, preventing context from being mixed between projects.

How much autonomy does it have?

It handles routine technical decisions and can inspect, implement, test and review within the authorized workspace and scope. Autonomy never silently expands the objective or permissions.

When does it request approval?

Before production, publication, DNS, credentials, costs, reduced security, or destructive and hard-to-reverse operations. Local, reversible and well-defined actions proceed autonomously.

Does it monitor the computer continuously?

No. C7ES installs no daemon and does not change the power plan. Capacity is checked on demand before heavy work; on a limited computer, stages can be serialized and resumed from checkpoints.

Does it guarantee token and time savings?

It does not guarantee quotas or make hardware faster. It is designed to reduce unnecessary context, repetition and rework while reusing valid stages. Actual gains depend on the task and environment.
