07 · A local engineering layer for Codex

Active locally · v1.0.0

C7 Engineering System

I turn everyday tasks into a proportional, verifiable and reversible workflow.

I built a local layer that organizes context, risk, validation and resumption so different projects remain under control.

  • Local-first
  • 9 skills
  • Validated rollback

Different projects need proportional context, rigor and cost.

Concurrent work can mix rules, repeat investigation or overload a limited computer. I built C7ES to identify the right project, preserve decisions and scale depth with risk.

  • Project separation
  • Risk from R0 to R4
  • Checkpoint continuity

A short core with knowledge loaded on demand.

The system combines compact global instructions, nine progressive skills, five focused profiles, deterministic protections and an indexed local library.

  • Seven-lens quorum
  • Progressive routing
  • No resident service

Activation required tests, comparison and a real rollback.

Version 1.0.0 passed 42 Pester tests, 24 checks in the installed environment, comparative evaluations and byte-for-byte restoration in a disposable profile.

  • 42 Pester tests
  • 24 installed checks
  • Byte-for-byte rollback

Work adapts to the available computer.

Before heavy tasks, C7ES observes CPU, memory, paging and storage. On a limited host, it serializes stages, lowers priority and resumes valid cases without repeating calls.

  • No power-plan changes
  • Proportional concurrency
  • Savings through resumption

Version 1.0.0 is active, local and reversible.

The core, skills, profiles, rules and three deterministic protections are installed and verified. Everyday operation needs no external service, added subscription or recurring cost.

  • Local use
  • Versioned backup
  • Verifiable maintenance
