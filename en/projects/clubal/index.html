01 · Platform for organizing institutional operations

Evolving · New web version

ClubAL

Operational routines and information organized for the people who record, publish and access them.

ClubAL connects routine management, record lookup and operational information across its modules. The current Windows foundation remains available while the new Operador Web moves through development and validation.

  • Windows application
  • Operations, routines and consultation
  • New web version under validation
  • Integrated weather forecast
Role
Product direction and development of the local-first foundation, including responsibility boundaries and the evolution of Operador to the web.
Operational setting
Separate spreadsheets, paper records and messages produce conflicting versions. ClubAL centralizes records on the responsible computer and separates editing, publishing and access.
Contribution
I structured the product to separate those who record and publish from those who only consult. This division reduces unintended editing and maintains one local source of approved information.
Evidence and outcome
Screens with demonstration data document Operação, Rotinas, Consulta and the integrated weather forecast in the current Windows modules.
Status
Operação, Rotinas and Consulta continue in the Windows modules. Operador Web remains in development and validation with synthetic data.
