01 · Windows software for institutional routines

In development · assisted deployment

ClubAL

I bring PC records, display publishing and read-only access into one flow.

With ClubAL, I organize institutional routines on Windows, publish what displays need and provide read-only access.

  • Windows
  • Local-first
  • 3 modules

Operational information gets lost when every environment improvises.

Spreadsheets, paper and scattered messages create different versions of the same routine. I created ClubAL to centralize records on the responsible PC and separate editing from reading.

  • One source of record
  • Visibility by environment
  • Local operation

Each module handles one part of the work.

I organize the product into three modules: Operação records and publishes; Rotinas displays only approved information; Consulta provides authorized reading without opening editing.

  • Operação · edit and publish
  • Rotinas · display approved information
  • Consulta · read without editing

The product can be demonstrated and deployment remains assisted.

I maintain automated checks for Windows flows, offline mode and publishing rules. Installations, displays and physical networks are reviewed in their real environment.

  • Public data is fictional
  • No public download for now
  • Windows packages are not code-signed yet
