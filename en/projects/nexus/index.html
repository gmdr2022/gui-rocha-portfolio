04 · A Windows hub for local projects

Validated local tool · Windows

NEXUS

The right path appears before execution — without hunting through shortcuts.

NEXUS brings projects, paths and checks into one local hub that shows what is ready and what needs attention.

  • Interface-based setup
  • Tracked paths
  • Works without a connection
Role
Product direction and development of the local hub, including registration, safe path discovery and validations.
Problem
Each product has its own versions, tools and checks; the right path needs to appear before anything runs.
Solution
Projects and paths are added from the interface; NEXUS only adopts a newer alternative when the match is safe and unambiguous.
Evidence and outcome
Startup without a connection, interface-based registration and safe path discovery were validated in the local base.
Status
Projects and checks open in their development context, without a required login and without mixing product environments.
