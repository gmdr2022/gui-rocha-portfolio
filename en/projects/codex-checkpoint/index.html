03 · A local queue for validations and decisions

Local product · Windows

Codex Checkpoint

Pending work ready to resume at the right time, with essential decisions and information preserved.

Codex Checkpoint keeps validations, decisions and feedback available across work sessions.

  • No account
  • Durable queue
  • Backup and recovery
Role
Product direction and development of the native application, from the interface to the mechanisms that preserve pending work across sessions.
Starting point
Playtesting, visual review, device checks and authentication may depend on another time or environment. The queue records what is needed so work can resume without reconstructing its history.
Solution
Each checkpoint gathers enough information to resume the pending item and record a decision or actionable feedback.
Evidence and outcome
Card imports with duplicate-safe keys were validated in the local flow; the application also provides backup and recovery.
Status
Interface, command line and file integration form one product. Current versions are not code-signed yet, and that limit remains clearly stated.
