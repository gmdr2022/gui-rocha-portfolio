In development · Phase 8

Maeve Roscaern

Dun Caerwyn turns blood, memory and choices into persistent marks.

Maeve Roscaern is an original single-player RPG developed at a compact scale for a solo production.

  • Single-player experience
  • Persistent choices
  • No mandatory account or server
Role
Creative direction, systems design and individual implementation of the prototype.
Game
Dun Caerwyn, House Roscaern and its systems make body, blood and memory shape Maeve’s journey, narrative and combat.
Production
Compact areas, modular kits and concentrated detail preserve the mood without promising a world larger than solo production can support.
Status
Light combo, dodge and Blood First now form part of Phase 8. The next stage focuses on playtesting and adjustments to combat feel, pace and legibility.
