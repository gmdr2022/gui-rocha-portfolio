02 · A dark fantasy RPG built for solo production

In development · Phase 8

Maeve Roscaern

In Dun Caerwyn, I am building a story of blood, memory and choices that leave marks.

I am developing a single-player RPG in Unreal Engine 5.8 with compact areas, modular pieces and a scale suited to solo production.

  • Unreal Engine 5.8
  • Single-player
  • Offline-first

An adult protagonist in a world that records consequences.

I am building Dun Caerwyn, House Roscaern and systems where body, blood and memory shape Maeve’s journey, narrative and combat.

  • Original world and protagonist
  • Persistent choices
  • No mandatory account or server

The scale is designed for one person to finish.

I use compact areas, modular kits and concentrated detail to preserve the mood without promising a world larger than solo production can support.

  • Short, readable paths
  • Reusable pieces and characters
  • Artwork clearly labeled as concept

I appear as a possible entity in the world.

My image as The Architect is a conceptual easter egg about authorship and consequence. It inspires the project without claiming that the character is already playable.

  • My concept portrait
  • Narrative possibility
  • Concept is not presented as gameplay

The first combat slice has a technical foundation.

I have integrated light combo, dodge and Blood First into Phase 8. Technical gates passed; combat feel, rhythm and readability are still being evaluated.

  • Visuals are evolving
  • Clear C++ and Unreal responsibilities
  • Licenses revalidated before use
