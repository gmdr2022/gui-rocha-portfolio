Data and hosting

The code does not create profiles or receive form data. Cloudflare hosts and serves the files and may process technical request metadata under its own policies and operational needs. This portfolio does not add a database or public log access.

Cookies and local storage

The gui_consent cookie records for up to 180 days whether you chose essential storage only or allowed preferences. Theme and accessibility settings remain in localStorage only with permission; otherwise they last for the session. The service worker may cache public files for more resilient loading.

External links

WhatsApp, GitHub, email and project websites only receive a navigation when you choose to open the link. From that point, the selected service handles data under its own terms.

Your controls

The Cookies button lets you change your choice. The accessibility button adjusts text size, contrast, motion and font. You can also clear cookies, storage and cache in your browser settings.
